A '''sulfur cube''' is a [[bucket of Sulfur Cube|bucketable]] jumping [[passive mob]] that is found only in [[sulfur caves]]. It can absorb a full-sized [[block]] into itself, which immobilizes it and makes it receive higher [[Knockback (mechanic)|knockback]] from most attacks instead of damage. The block type a sulfur cube absorbs affects its bounciness and behavior, and some blocks cannot be absorbed, such as [[slab]]s and [[stairs]]. Blocks can be extracted using [[shears]], by replacing the block with a different one, or by killing the sulfur cube.

'''Small sulfur cubes''' spawn in groups of two when a large sulfur cube dies. They grow up over time similarly to [[babies]] and cannot absorb blocks.

== Spawning ==
Large sulfur cubes spawn naturally in [[sulfur caves]] at any light level.

Two small sulfur cubes spawn when a large sulfur cube dies, which can then grow up into large sulfur cubes; see {{Section link||Splitting}} below.

{{Mob spawn table}}

== Drops ==
Upon death, large sulfur cubes drop the block they absorbed and 1-2 [[experience]], while small sulfur cubes drop no experience.

== Behavior ==
Sulfur cubes are passive mobs that, by default, jump around aimlessly. In this state, they are susceptible to all sources of damage.

If a sulfur cube [[pathfind]]s into a block that can be [[climb]]ed, such as [[ladder]]s or [[vines]], the sulfur cube climbs the block. Sulfur cubes with absorbed blocks that are launched are also capable of climbing.{{Only|java}}

=== Splitting ===
Sulfur cubes come in two sizes: small and large. When a large sulfur cube dies, it splits into two small cubes, unless the death was caused by its own explosion. If a small sulfur cube dies, it does not split and drops nothing. When a named large sulfur cube is killed and splits, both small cubes retain the name as with other cube mobs.

Small sulfur cubes grow into large ones after 20 minutes, similarly to [[baby]] mobs. [[Slimeball]]s can be used to tempt and feed small sulfur cubes, which decreases their remaining growth time. They can also be fed a [[golden dandelion]] to prevent them from growing.

=== Bucketing ===
A large sulfur cube can be picked up by using an empty [[bucket]] on it to create a [[bucket of sulfur cube]]. If a named sulfur cube is bucketed, the name of the item becomes "Bucket of ''<name>''" (the sulfur cube's name){{only|bedrock}} or only the cube's name{{Only|java}}. 

If the bucketed sulfur cube has an absorbed block, the bucket of sulfur cube item tooltip displays the name of the absorbed block.

A bucketed sulfur cube no longer counts toward the [[mob cap]] and does not [[despawn]]. However, if the bucketed sulfur cube is split into two small cubes, the small cubes can despawn.<ref>{{bug|MC-308361||Small sulfur cubes that split from bucketed sulfur cubes can despawn|WAI}}</ref>

=== <span class="anchor" id="Archetypes"></span> Absorbing blocks ===
{{Redirect|Sulfur cube archetype|the definition of sulfur cube archetypes in data packs|Sulfur cube archetype definition}}
Large sulfur cubes have the ability to absorb some [[block]]s, mostly [[interaction box|full-sized]] ones. It can absorb a block [[Item (entity)|dropped]] on the ground (unless {{cmd|gamerule mob_griefing}} is set to <code>false</code>), and also can be fed a block by {{control|interacting}} with it. Sulfur cubes move toward absorbable blocks dropped on the ground, and they also follow players holding an applicable block in their hand. Small sulfur cubes cannot absorb blocks. A [[dispenser]] can also be used to feed a block to a sulfur cube if the cube's hitbox intersects the block space directly in front of the dispenser.

They can also absorb any block using commands.

Any method of feeding a block to a sulfur cube can also be done on a sulfur cube that has already absorbed a block, which changes the block inside it and drops its previously-absorbed block as an item.

When a sulfur cube has absorbed a block, it stops moving and its absorbed block is visible inside of it. The absorbed block can be removed from a sulfur cube by using [[shears]] on it, which drops the block as an item. Dispensers with shears can also be used to remove the block.

Absorbed blocks in sulfur cubes have the same visual properties as when placed down, including MERS textures with [[Vibrant Visuals]]. Froglights, for example, glow inside the sulfur cube, but they do not actually light the environment gameplay-wise. Throwing a [[splash potion of Invisibility]] on a sulfur cube causes it to show only the absorbed block.

Sulfur cubes can absorb [[Anvil#Renaming|named]] blocks; the block remains named if removed, but the name of the block is not displayed above the sulfur cube.

==== Special archetypes ====
Sulfur cubes that have absorbed specific functional blocks can have those functions applied to them. 
*[[TNT]] has the explosive archetype. When a sulfur cube with the explosive archetype is ignited manually, its fuse time is set to {{convert|120|game tick|second}}. When ignited by another explosion, it is set to be a random number between {{convert|15|game tick|second}} and {{convert|44|game tick|second}}, inclusive.
** Sulfur cubes with ignited TNT cannot be damaged, be captured in a [[bucket]], or have their absorbed block [[shear]]ed. When the sulfur cube explodes, no small sulfur cubes are spawned.
** Sulfur cubes with the explosive archetype can be ignited in the same ways that TNT can be [[TNT#Activation|activated]] and more, including: 
*** direct usage of [[flint and steel]] by a player or [[dispenser]], or usage of a [[fire charge]] by a player;
*** causing any [[fire damage]] to the sulfur cube ([[fire]], [[lava]], flaming projectile, [[campfire]] or [[magma block]]);
**** this works even if the mob has [[Fire Resistance]] on it;
*** being hit by a weapon with [[Fire Aspect]];
*** any other [[explosion]] nearby, including explosions from other sulfur cubes;
*** a [[redstone]] signal. TNT activates if the center of sulfur cube's hitbox is in such a block that a TNT would be activated if it was a placed block instead.
****TNT sulfur cubes are also primed if they occupy the same position as a redstone component that's currently emitting a signal (a [[comparator]], for example), even though that position might not be necessarily powered.
*[[Magma block]]s have the hot archetype. Sulfur cubes with the hot archetype damage nearby mobs, acting as if they touched magma.

==== Damage resistance ====
A sulfur cube that has absorbed a block is resistant to the following forms of damage:
* [[Melee attack]]s
* [[Projectile]]s
* [[Falling block]]s, including [[anvil]]s and [[stalactite]]s
* [[Explosion]]s, except their own
* [[Fall damage]], including onto [[stalagmite]]s
* [[Freezing]]
* Contact with [[cactus]] or [[sweet berry bushes]]
* [[Magma block]]s and sulfur cubes with the hot archetype
Other sources of damage, such as [[fire]], [[suffocation]], [[cramming]], or the damage [[effect]]s, can damage or kill the sulfur cube as normal.

Some hostile mobs attack the sulfur cube even while it has absorbed a block, such as [[zoglin]]s, the [[wither]] and [[warden]]s. Although the sulfur cube is immune to the warden's melee damage, they can be killed by its [[sonic boom]]. Additionally, tamed [[wolves]] attack a sulfur cube that their owner attacks.

==== Launching ====
When affected by [[Knockback (mechanic)|knockback]], the sulfur cube is launched and is affected by [[gravity]] and [[physics]]. This causes it to behave and bounce like a ball.

The direction and trajectory in which a sulfur cube is launched is the opposite of where on its body it is hit.{{Needs testing|May not be implemented yet in Bedrock Edition.}}

This diagram describes how hitting a sulfur cube on different portions of its body launches it.
{| class="wikitable"
! colspan="1" |Sulfur Cube Launching Portions
|- style="vertical-align:middle;"
|[[File:Sulfur Cube Launching Guide.png|500px]]

The force at which a sulfur cube is launched is based on the amount of damage the attack does. For example, hitting it with a [[diamond sword]] launches it farther than punching it without an item. The [[Knockback]] and [[Punch]] enchantments also apply additional power to the launch.

Sulfur cubes that are in the air can be knocked farther upward when launched. This is particularly notable {{in|JE}}, where this behavior differs from the usual [[Knockback (mechanic)#Versus airborne entities|airborne knockback mechanics]].

Depending on the absorbed block, sulfur cubes exhibit different properties when hit.

== Trivia ==
* Prior to sulfur cubes, the idea of [[slime]]s being used as a ball appeared as a brief visual gag in [[Village & Pillage: Official Trailer]].
* During [[Minecraft Live – March 2026]], it was stated by [[Agnes Larsson]] that the face of the cube is meant to have an ambiguous expression.

* Sulfur cubes with custom sizes split into smaller sulfur cubes until they get to normal size.
